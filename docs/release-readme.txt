Deckhand

Requirements: an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.

Drag Deckhand into Applications, then open it. Homebrew, Python, FFmpeg and
developer tools are not needed to run the app.

Choose a music folder, Apple Music, or a Rekordbox XML export to get started.
Your playlists and ratings save in Deckhand. Check playlists to include on
your drive, then click Sync. Deckhand does not copy your library to a server.

For Apple Music imports, macOS may ask for Music or Automation permission.
Audio files must be accessible on this Mac; streaming-only tracks cannot
be exported. Add Music and import history are in Library Settings.
